FishBuy shopping guide

For every product you shortlist, save the listing link, variant, size and date checked. Those four details make it easier to compare similar items or revisit a link that redirects.

Compare shoes and clothing for fit

For sneaker sizing, measure both feet in centimeters and use the longer foot with the seller’s size chart. Compare sneaker insole length only with another insole measurement. If you wear thick socks, check what the fit is like with them.

For hoodies, compare flat chest width, shoulder width, sleeve length and body length with a garment you own. For trousers, check waist, rise and inseam. For a matching set, confirm the size options for both pieces.

Review product links and QC photos

Open each FishBuy product find again before ordering. Match the color, size, quantity and photographed details to the exact listing variant. If a link has expired, record that before substituting a similar item.

If warehouse quality-control photos are available, inspect sneaker stitching, outsole wear, zipper condition, cap adjustment hardware and jewelry clasp details. Ask for a ruler photo if the measurements matter to the fit.

Compare delivery estimates by destination

Use your delivery country and postal code when comparing available routes. Save the parcel weight, box dimensions, estimated transit time and insurance terms beside each quote.

For a first international parcel, check whether the shipping quote uses actual or dimensional weight. Compare that estimate with the full delivered cost, and read the return window before approving dispatch.

Frequently asked questions

What is a FishBuy spreadsheet?

A FishBuy spreadsheet is a shopping reference used to group product links by category and record details like size, color and photo notes. This guide directs readers to HipobuyFind for category browsing.

Where do the category links go?

They open the corresponding categories on HipobuyFind. Check the destination listing for current product details and available options.

Can quality-control photos verify authenticity?

Quality-control photos can help you check visible details, measurements and condition. Verify authenticity through an authorized seller or a qualified authentication service.

How do I estimate the total delivered cost?

Add the item amount, domestic shipping, service fees, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties. Recheck the estimate for your destination and parcel dimensions before paying.
